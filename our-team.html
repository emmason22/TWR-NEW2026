Our Team

Chad Davenport, Founder

Chad is a U.S. Navy veteran who learned the importance of showing up for others while serving his country. During his time in the Navy, he helped in the evacuation after Mount Pinatubo erupted in the Philippines and provided humanitarian support to San Francisco after the 1989 earthquake. For the past 30 years, that same spirit of compassion has guided him as he organizes food and clothing drives, hosts annual Toy Drives for local families, prepares home-cooked meals, and personally delivers care, hope, and connection to people experiencing homelessness in Dallas.

Carlos Contreras, Director of Volunteer Services

Carlos is a second-generation contributor to Tonight We Ride, proudly following in the footsteps of his father, Ray. A committed family man, he has raised his children to volunteer alongside him, ensuring that the spirit of service continues through the next generation. For over 20 years, Carlos has played a key role in organizing donation sorting and logistics, coordinating canned goods drives, and fundraising for local school athletic programs. He is also a familiar and beloved presence at our annual Toy Drive, where he has joyfully taken on the role of Santa Claus for countless children and families.

Ricky Fandrick, Director of Digital Experience

Ricky Fandrick brings a steady, behind-the-scenes commitment to helping people feel seen, supported, and connected. He joined the Tonight We Ride team in 2025 and serves as Director of Digital Experience, helping shape the organization's digital presence so its mission of dignity, compassion, and relationship-first outreach reaches more people in meaningful ways.

His work focuses on creating clear, welcoming experiences that reflect the heart of the organization and make it easier for supporters, partners, and neighbors in need to engage with the mission. In every part of his involvement, Ricky Fandrick brings consistency, care, and a belief that even digital spaces can help build trust, connection, and hope.

Media & Content Coordinator

Help Tell Stories That Change Lives

Tonight We Ride is looking for a creative volunteer who can help capture, create, and share the stories behind our mission. From outreach events and veteran support initiatives to community partnerships and fundraising campaigns, you'll help showcase the impact being made throughout Dallas.

Whether you're a photographer, videographer, social media enthusiast, graphic designer, or simply someone passionate about making a difference, we'd love to connect.

Skills We're Looking For:

  • Photography & videography
  • Social media content creation
  • Graphic design
  • Storytelling and writing
  • Website content updates
  • Creative ideas that help grow our impact

Blake Ingram, Impact Systems & Scheduling Coordinator

Blake Ingram joined Tonight We Ride in November 2025 with a deep commitment to helping people feel seen through hands-on outreach and compassionate service. Known for his dedication and genuine care, he is passionate about showing up consistently for those in need throughout the Dallas community.

Blake also volunteers with I Belong, where he supports music therapy programs for young adults with autism and Down syndrome. He brings heart, reliability, and a sincere desire to build meaningful connections through every act of service. Blake is proud to contribute to Tonight We Ride's mission of delivering hope, dignity, and connection across Dallas.
