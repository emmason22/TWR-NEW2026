Veteran Support Programs

Comms Up

A Tonight We Ride Veteran Connectivity Program
Through Comms Up, Tonight We Ride provides mobile phones and communication access to veterans experiencing hardship, helping them reconnect with family, services, and opportunity.

L.I.F.T.

Logistics & Intervention For Teammates
Through the L.I.F.T. program, Tonight We Ride helps veterans overcome transportation barriers by coordinating rides to medical appointments, support services, and other essential resources.

Because sometimes the difference between getting help and going without is simply having a way to get there.

Veteran Resource Navigation

Many veterans simply need help finding the right door. Through our Veteran Resource Navigation effort, Tonight We Ride works with trusted local partners to help veterans reconnect with essential support, including:

  • VA Healthcare
  • Housing Assistance Programs
  • Mental Health Resources
  • Employment Services
  • Veteran Support Organizations

Sometimes the hardest part is simply knowing where to start — and we're here to help guide that first step.
