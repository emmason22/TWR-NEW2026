Tonight We Ride Names Rabbit Hat Promotions as Public Relations Partner

Dallas, Texas — Tonight We Ride is proud to announce Rabbit Hat Promotions as its public relations partner, supporting the organization's efforts to expand awareness, strengthen community engagement, and grow support for its mission across North Texas.

Founded by Chad Davenport, Tonight We Ride is a Dallas-based nonprofit dedicated to serving individuals and families in need through compassionate, relationship-driven outreach. The organization's work includes homeless outreach, veteran support, crisis relief, community partnerships, and its annual toy drive, all centered on restoring dignity, hope, and human connection.

Through this new partnership, Rabbit Hat Promotions will support Tonight We Ride with media relations, strategic communications, and storytelling designed to elevate the organization's voice and increase visibility for its outreach efforts and community impact.

"Tonight We Ride has always been about showing up for people, building trust, and creating real connection through service," said Chad Davenport, Founder of Tonight We Ride. "We're excited to work with Rabbit Hat Promotions to help share our story, grow our reach, and bring more people into the mission."

As Tonight We Ride continues to expand its impact, the organization remains committed to meeting people where they are and serving with consistency, compassion, and purpose. The partnership with Rabbit Hat Promotions marks an important step in building greater awareness and support for that work.

About Tonight We Ride

Tonight We Ride is a Dallas-based nonprofit founded by Chad Davenport and dedicated to serving neighbors in need through direct outreach, community partnerships, crisis support, veteran assistance, and seasonal giving initiatives. Built on compassion and consistency, Tonight We Ride works to make a lasting difference one person at a time.

About Rabbit Hat Promotions

Rabbit Hat Promotions is a public relations and promotions company focused on helping brands, artists, events, and organizations grow their visibility through strategic communications, media outreach, and creative storytelling. With a passion for connecting people and purpose, Rabbit Hat Promotions works to amplify voices that make an impact in their communities.
