Rabbit Hat Promotions Named Public Relations Agency of Record for Tonight We Ride

Dallas, Texas - Rabbit Hat Promotions is proud to announce its new role as the public relations agency for Tonight We Ride, the Dallas-based nonprofit organization dedicated to serving individuals and families in need through compassionate, relationship-driven outreach.

Tonight We Ride has built its mission around restoring dignity, hope, and human connection through direct support for neighbors facing hardship. Its work includes homeless outreach, veteran support, crisis relief, community partnerships, and its long-running annual toy drive that has become a meaningful tradition in Dallas.

As PR partner, Rabbit Hat Promotions will lead media relations, storytelling, visibility campaigns, and strategic communications designed to amplify Tonight We Ride's mission, deepen community awareness, and help drive support for its outreach efforts throughout North Texas.

Founded on the belief that meaningful change begins with presence, Tonight We Ride focuses on meeting people where they are and building trust through ongoing support. From emergency response and veteran outreach to its annual holiday giving efforts, the organization continues to create impact across Dallas by putting people first.

"Rabbit Hat understands how to connect heart, community, and culture through storytelling," said Chad Davenport, founder of Tonight We Ride. "We are thrilled to have them alongside us as we continue growing our mission and reaching more people who want to get involved."

Rabbit Hat Promotions is known for supporting clients in creative industries through communications, branding, and special events, making the partnership a natural fit for Tonight We Ride's community-centered voice and grassroots spirit.

About Tonight We Ride

Tonight We Ride is a Dallas-based nonprofit outreach organization focused on dignity, consistency, and practical support for neighbors in crisis. Through homeless outreach, veteran support, crisis relief, community partnerships, and community-driven events, Tonight We Ride works to change the world one person at a time.
