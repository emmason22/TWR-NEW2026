Privacy Policy

Tonight We Ride respects your privacy. We may collect information you submit in forms, such as your name, phone, email, and message details, to respond to requests and coordinate outreach.

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers or community partners only when needed to provide requested support.

You may request updates or deletion of your information by contacting info@tonightweride.org.

Donation Disclosure

Donations support outreach operations, direct support activities, events, and program needs serving the Dallas community. Unless otherwise specified, donations may be allocated where needed most.

Tonight We Ride has applied for recognition as a federal tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3) and is currently pending IRS determination.

At this time, contributions should not be assumed tax-deductible unless and until 501(c)(3) status is formally approved.

Donors should retain receipts and consult a qualified tax professional regarding deductibility and tax treatment.

If a donation error occurs, contact us at info@tonightweride.org.
