More than one night

Music opens the door. Community carries the mission.

For more than two decades, the Toy Drive has made holiday giving feel immediate, welcoming, and unmistakably Dallas.

Guests gather for live music and bring new, unwrapped toys or monetary donations. The volunteer team then delivers the collected gifts and needed items to Dallas Life and The Family Place, connecting one memorable event to direct community care.

Sponsors help underwrite the experience, amplify the invitation, and turn high-visibility event moments into more support for the people the drive serves.
