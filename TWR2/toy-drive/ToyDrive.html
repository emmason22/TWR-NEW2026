Tonight We Ride Presents

Chad D's 27th Annual Toy Drive

For 27 years, Chad Davenport's Annual Toy Drive has brought the Dallas music community together to make the holidays brighter for neighbors in crisis.

Every toy and dollar supports Dallas Life Homeless Shelter and The Family Place. On Christmas Day, the Toy Drive’s longtime volunteer team delivers the donations to both organizations, with Santa joining to create a memorable holiday experience for the families served.
