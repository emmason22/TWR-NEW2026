Crisis Relief Support

When the unexpected happens — whether in our own backyard or across the world — Tonight We Ride responds by rallying people, raising resources, and directing help where it’s needed most.

Over the years, our community has come together to support relief efforts during crises such as the Texas Hill Country floods, the California wildfires, and the earthquake in Haiti, helping provide assistance to families facing sudden hardship.

TWR Crisis Concert Series

When crisis strikes — whether it’s a storm, fire, or local tragedy — our community shows up.

The TWR Crisis Concert Series brings people together through live music and generosity to support families and neighbors during difficult times. Each event raises funds and collects essential supplies such as food, clothing, toiletries, and emergency resources for those impacted.

How You Can Help

  • Attend a Concert
    Join us and be part of the community response.
  • Donate
    Contribute essential supplies or make a financial donation to support relief efforts.
  • Spread the Word
    Invite friends and help keep the music — and the mission — moving forward. Every ticket, donation, and song played helps restore hope when it’s needed most.

Together, we turn music into momentum — and compassion into relief.
